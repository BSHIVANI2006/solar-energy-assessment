How the system works

Input: Location, usable rooftop area, peak sunlight hours, electricity tariff and monthly bill.

Processing: The application estimates system capacity, monthly energy output, savings and avoided emissions using transparent formulas.

Output: A results dashboard with solar capacity, generation, savings, demand coverage and CO₂ reduction estimates.

Important assumptions

  • For this prototype, 100 sq. ft. of usable roof area is assumed per 1 kW of solar capacity.
  • A performance ratio of 0.75 is used to account approximately for system losses.
  • Generation is estimated as capacity × peak sunlight hours × 30 days × performance ratio.
  • Financial savings assume all estimated solar generation offsets electricity billed at the entered tariff. Actual savings depend on self-consumption, net-metering rules, fixed charges and utility policy.
  • The CO₂ estimate uses the emission factor entered by the user. It is an indicative estimate, not a verified emissions audit.
  • This tool is an educational screening prototype, not a structural, electrical or professional solar feasibility survey.
